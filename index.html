Our Story

Decor that adds beauty to every celebration.

Sakthi Decors brings together beautiful decorative pieces, festive collections, and thoughtful gifting ideas designed to make homes and auspicious celebrations more special.

Founded with deep reverence for South Indian traditions—from the resonant glow of morning pooja vilakkus to the joyous gathering of Navaratri Kolu—our atelier blends age-old iconography with fine laser-cut craftsmanship, creating pieces that families treasure for generations.
